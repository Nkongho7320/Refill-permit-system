Permit Lifecycle / Permit-Lebenszyklus
Step 1 · Monday (before 15:00)
Contractor submits Tätigkeitsbeschreibung
§1–§4 filled electronically. Submitted to SiGeKo for Tuesday review.
Step 2 · Tuesday – Coordination Meeting
SiGeKo reviews, adds conditions (§8)
Checklist verified. Special conditions entered. Adjacent trades coordinated. Permit approved or revision requested.
Step 3 · Before work starts
Safety briefing + worker signatures (§9)
Foreman briefs all workers on site. Each worker signs. No signature = no deployment.
Step 4 · Work start
Dual release signatures (§11)
SiGeKo/BL and Foreman both sign. Permit becomes active. Displayed at work location.
Week active · NOW
Work in progress – Permit valid
Gas measurements at set intervals. Daily site checks. Any material change → new permit required immediately.
Every Tuesday – Renewal
Tuesday meeting: Re-check + renewal or closure
SiGeKo reviews status. Re-check: gas measurement · area clear · personnel unchanged. If work continues: new signatures. If complete: close-out (§12).
Work completion
Close-out signatures (§12) + archiving (§13)
Foreman + SiGeKo sign off. Area confirmed safe. 5-year archiving initiated.
